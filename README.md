SolarGreen is an application with great potential, as it addresses one of the key challenges of the 21st century — energy, renewable sources, and their recycling. The issue is not only environmental but also economic and socially essential, since without an effective system for managing end-of-life photovoltaic panels, we risk severe environmental pollution, loss of valuable resources, and unsustainable development.

Through SolarGreen, users gain access to an easy-to-use platform that allows them to submit their panels for recycling — thus not only protecting the environment but also contributing to the circular economy. In partnership with certified recycling companies, the application ensures that the process is transparent, efficient, and financially beneficial for all participants.
